# 🌱 CarbonWise

> Empowering individuals to measure, understand, and reduce their carbon footprint through an intelligent and user-friendly platform.

---

# 📌 Problem Statement

Climate change is one of the most pressing global challenges, and a significant contributor is the increasing carbon footprint generated by daily human activities. However, many individuals lack awareness of how their lifestyle choices impact the environment and often do not have access to simple tools that help them calculate, track, and reduce their carbon emissions.

There is a need for an accessible platform that enables users to estimate their carbon footprint, visualize their environmental impact, and receive personalized recommendations to adopt more sustainable habits.

---

# 💡 Solution

CarbonWise is a full-stack web application designed to help users calculate, monitor, and reduce their carbon footprint.

The platform collects information about users' daily activities such as transportation, electricity usage, and lifestyle habits to estimate their carbon emissions. It provides insightful analytics, tracks historical data, and offers AI-powered recommendations for reducing environmental impact.

By combining data visualization, secure user management, and sustainability insights, CarbonWise encourages users to make environmentally responsible decisions.

# ⚙ Installation

### Clone Repository



